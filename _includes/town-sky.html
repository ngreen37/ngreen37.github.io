{%- comment -%} ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ THE TOWN SKY — the whole town lives under one sky, on every page. ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 2026-07-12 (Nate: "the other pages, I thought we were going to give them day/dusk/night/dawn themes, like the PJCC page? Let's really make that a thing"). Up to now the hour existed in exactly two places: the PJCC hero's full sky, and a 0.03–0.07 colour wash over everything else — technically town-wide, invisible in practice. This is the real thing: a fixed, full-viewport sky sitting BEHIND every page (z-index:-1, so it paints over the flat body colour and under every card, header and footer). You see it in the gutters, above and below the content — the town is now a place the pages sit inside, instead of a colour they're tinted with. It's STATIC MARKUP, not JS-built: `sky-` and `cloud-` are already on before first paint (see town-weather.html), so the sky is correct on the very first frame with no flash and no layout work. Stacking, and it matters — Nate: "the stars should always fall BEHIND the moon. Unless it's a shooting star": stars (1) → orb, sun or moon (2) → shooting stars (3) → clouds (4) So a shooting star can streak across the moon's face, and cloud can pass in front of the lot. Nothing else may sit between the stars and the orb. PERF (the 2026-07-11 lag rules, which this obeys to the letter): the container is position:fixed + overflow:hidden + contain, so it is capped at the VIEWPORT and never grows with the document. Every moving part animates transform/opacity ONLY — no background-position anywhere, no filter on any wrapper of an animating child. Off entirely under reduce-flourish / prefers-reduced-motion, which leaves the static sky (gradient + orb) exactly as it is. ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} 1 · the stars. Night only. Two of every handful twinkle; the rest are fixed points, because a sky where every star pulses reads as a screensaver. {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} 2 · the orb: one element, four looks. The day sun grows rays; the night moon gets a crater shadow. Sits ABOVE the stars, always. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} 3 · shooting stars. Night only, and the one thing allowed in FRONT of the moon. Three of them on long, deliberately coprime cycles (29s · 43s · 67s) with a streak that lasts barely a second — so they never fall in a pattern you can read, and most of the time the sky is simply still. No JS: the sky is idle markup and the compositor does the rest. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} 4 · the cloud deck. Cover is a daily roll from the town clock (PJCC_TIME.clouds() → html.cloud-0..3), so the whole town gets the same sky, and it's decorrelated from the rain roll — a clear day can still be a cloudy one, and a starry night can still have cloud crossing it. Two banks drifting at different speeds (transform only) so the deck never repeats visibly. {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} 4b · the shape clouds (2026-07-13 Nate: "like when you are with a friend pointing at clouds — 'that one looks like a rook'"). Two of them: a rook and a dog, VAGUELY — soft puffs that only suggest the outline. Rare twice over: each only exists on ~1 day in 4 (shape-rook / shape-dog, rolled from the same day seed as the rest of the weather in town-weather.html, so the whole town points at the same cloud), and within the day each spends half its long cycle off-screen. Parked at left:100% so with animation off they simply never enter. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} 4c · the LONG-cloud day (2026-07-15 Nate: "sometimes the clouds get really loooong, like clouds do — a little more transparent than the fluffy ones"). Two stratus streaks, gated by html.shape-long (~1 day in 4). {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} 5 · the far horizon: the town's own light, low and warm, along the foot of the sky. It's what stops the backdrop reading as "a gradient" and starts it reading as "somewhere". {%- endcomment -%}
