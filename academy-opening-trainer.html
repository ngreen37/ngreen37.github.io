So how about a trainer where you get into the main line (and variations like Lions Jaw, etc) so you can hit it hard… Build it out as much as possible, starting with PIRC-ONLY - and ONLY WITH BLACK for the user… Do 6 different levels - true beginner opponent all the way to perfect play." ⚠⚠ THIS IS NOT *TABIYA*, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS THE WHOLE IDEA. /games/pirc-protocol/ (Tabiya) is a BOOK DRILL: it asks for the book move and it ends when the book ends. That teaches you a move order. It does not teach you the opening, because the opening is the position it leaves you in — and a student who can recite fourteen plies and then has no idea what to do on move eight has not learned the Pirc. So this room is two halves welded together, and the weld is the point: · THE WALK-IN — both sides are scripted through the chosen variation, so you reliably ARRIVE at the tabiya. Play the book move and it advances; play something else and it says so and lets you try again. Nothing is scored, nothing is timed. · THE PLAY-OUT — the instant the book runs out, White is handed to the engine at the level you picked and you play the middlegame you just built. THIS is the lesson. ⭐ WHY THE PAGE OWNS NO CHESS. Move generation is pjcc-chess.js (the perft-verified referee, shared with every other board on the site) and the opponent is pjcc-gauntlet-engine.js (Stockfish, with the referee re-validating every move it returns and a negamax fallback when it cannot load). The repertoire is assets/js/pjcc-pirc-book.js, replayed through the referee at load. This file is a BOARD and a STATE MACHINE — every chess claim it makes is proved somewhere else, by something a test can run without a browser. [[accuracy-above-all]] ⚠ THE STUDENT IS ALWAYS BLACK. That is Nate's constraint, and it is load-bearing in more places than it looks: the board is drawn flipped, the book alternates from a WHITE first ply, `USER` is a constant rather than a setting, and every line in the book ends on a Black move so the hand-off never hands White two moves in a row. If a white repertoire is ever wanted, it is a new book file and a `side` flag — not a tweak to this page. ⚠ MARKED IN DEVELOPMENT, DELIBERATELY, AND IT IS PLAYABLE ANYWAY. The Opening Trainer

In Development The Pirc Defense, as Black. More openings — and more of this one — are coming.

Most opening trainers stop the moment the book stops, which is the moment the game actually starts. This one walks you into the line, then makes you play it. Pick how White comes at you, pick who you are playing, and go.

{%- comment -%} THE IDEA COMES BEFORE THE MOVES. An opening is a posture, not a move list, and a student who reaches move fourteen without knowing what the position WANTS has memorized a phone number. The text is `PJCCPircBook.CREED`; it is written in the voice of the character who owns this opening in canon and signed by nobody — see the long note over TEACHER in pjcc-pirc-book.js for why the name is not printed. {%- endcomment -%}
