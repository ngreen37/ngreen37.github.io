The Opening Trainer

In Development The Pirc Defense — as Black, or from the other chair. More openings — and more of this one — are coming.

Most opening trainers stop the moment the book stops, which is the moment the game actually starts. This one walks you into the line, then makes you play it. Pick how White comes at you, pick which end of the board you are sitting at, pick who you are playing, and go.
