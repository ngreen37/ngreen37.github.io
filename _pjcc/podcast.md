# PJCC / McPuppy — Podcast Notes

*Private working notes (this folder isn't published). Format ideas, captured from the shows I like.*

## Format & vibe — what to borrow
1. **YMH (Your Mom's House)** — a soundboard; laughing in the back; inside jokes that build over time; irreverent. **No current events.**
2. **Tim Dillon** — the background/set presence; sunglasses; irreverent.
3. **Dan Soder** — a basic music intro (simple, recognizable).
4. **MSSP (Matt & Shane's Secret Podcast)** — the "switch to the Patreon" move; low quality that *increases* (the bit where the audio/production visibly steps up when you cross over).
5. **Rick Rubin** — direct interview questions with little-to-no response from the interviewer (let the guest carry it; the host nearly disappears).
