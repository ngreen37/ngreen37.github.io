How to Play

  • Six attempts to crack a five-letter code word
  • Green = right letter, right spot  ·  Amber = right letter, wrong spot
  • Type on the on-screen keys or your keyboard; Enter to submit
  • Crack the code and the intercepted transmission decrypts itself — a new fragment of the file
  • Words are chess terms (Clearance: Delta) and PJCC field codes (Clearance: Omega)

Modes

  • Daily — one fixed intercept per day, shared by every operative
  • Endless — keep cracking; build a decryption streak

Companion to the Dead Drop and Classified files. Every word you crack unredacts another line of the archive — chess vocabulary and the Subject Zero mystery, one cipher at a time.
