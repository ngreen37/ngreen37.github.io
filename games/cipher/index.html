How to Play

  • Green = right letter, right spot  ·  Amber = right letter, wrong spot. Crack the code and the dispatch decrypts itself
  • Pick a pack — Chess · Openings · Endgame · PJCC Field Codes. Daily = one shared code a day · Endless keeps cracking · Weekly = a multi-word cryptogram
  • Crack three in a row to earn a 🔑 hint token (reveals a letter, at a score cost); your score rewards fewer guesses and faster time
  • After a solve, hit ⛓ Share for an emoji result card

Words are chess terms and PJCC field codes — every crack unredacts another line of the archive, and the weekly cryptogram unlocks a full intercepted dispatch. Scores log to your dossier and the leaderboards.
