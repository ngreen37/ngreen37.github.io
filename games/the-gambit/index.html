Lay it down. See what the board gives back.

Every game you play earns you something — credits, collectables, hard-won gear from the Quartermaster. The Gambit is the one place you can give it up. Offer your points, your money, your rarest finds to the board… and sometimes the board answers with something far greater.

How to play. Offer what you've earned — credits or a collectable — and let the board decide. Courage tilts the odds; you never stake real money. (Opening soon.)
