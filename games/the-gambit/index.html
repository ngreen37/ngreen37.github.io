Lay it down. See what the board gives back.

Every game you play earns you something — credits, collectables, hard-won gear from the Quartermaster. The Gambit is the one place you can give it up. Offer your points, your money, your rarest finds to the board… and sometimes the board answers with something far greater.

The wager

  • Offer what you've earned. Stake credits (your in-game money), or lay down a collectable — an avatar, a title, a board skin from the Quartermaster.
  • It's a chance, not a trade. Sometimes you walk away with nothing. Sometimes the board gives back far more than you put in.
  • Courage tilts the odds. Give up your best item — or 85%+ of your credits — and the odds lean hard in your favor. Small, safe offerings barely move the needle.
  • The point isn't winning. It's the willingness to let go of what you cling to. Goals are 5% strategy, 95% mindset. Assume it's already done — then follow the dog.

A values-first take on risk: the reward isn't here to teach you to gamble — and you will never stake real money. It's here to reward the one thing most people can't do: give up the very best thing they have and trust the path anyway. Built to plug straight into the economy you already use — the credits and collectables you earn at the Quartermaster are exactly what you'll lay on the altar. Nothing to offer yet — when it opens, the dispatch hears first.
