Across the water, everyone speaks Japanese and the board has nine files. The island's game is taught by playing — led by Kaede, with her brother Matsu helping and Auston, their first student, at the board beside you: the codex, the drops, the mate. Late lessons add real 詰将棋 tsume (forced mate-in-3 and mate-in-5, where the King fights back with its own hand), and a full 9×9 match vs the island AI unlocks once you've solved enough. Your decode progress and rank log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
