How to Play

  • A shogi piece sits in the middle of the board — forward is up (▲)
  • Tap every square that piece can legally move to, then press Check
  • Green = right · faded green = a move you missed · red = not a legal move
  • Ten pieces per round — read as many as you can

The pieces (駒)

  • 歩 Pawn — one step forward  ·  香 Lance — straight forward, any distance
  • 桂 Knight — jumps an L, forward only  ·  銀 Silver / 金 Gold — the generals
  • 角 Bishop — diagonals  ·  飛 Rook — straights  ·  王 King — one any way

When Princess falls through the pu-rin-se-suuu portal, she washes up where everyone speaks Japanese and the board has nine files. Learn the pieces here before the ferry runs again. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
