How to Play

  • The dock is jammed with crates and the ferry can't board
  • Drag a crate along its length — every crate slides in a straight line, like a rook
  • Crates can't pass through each other, so plan the order you clear them
  • Open a lane and slide Princess ♞ off the right edge onto the ferry
  • Solve in par moves or fewer to ace a crossing
  • Five crossings carry her to Shogi Island

On the world map, the Shogi Island ferry is marked "delayed" — and not for mechanical reasons. Every level is guaranteed solvable (checked by computer) and its par is the optimal number of moves.
