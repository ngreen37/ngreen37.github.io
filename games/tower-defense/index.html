How to Play

  • The CEO's pieces march the winding road toward Chess City — start with slow pawns, then bishops, knights, rooks, queens, and finally the King himself
  • Pick a defender from the rack, then tap a tile beside the road to build it. Towers fire on their own
  • Each piece you fell pays gold — spend it on more towers, or tap a placed tower to upgrade (up to L3) or sell it
  • Let an enemy reach the city and you lose hearts. Lose them all and the gates fall
  • Charge the 👑 Royal Decree by racking up kills — unleash it to blast every enemy on screen
  • Send next wave early for a gold bonus; bump the speed when you're confident

Your defenders

  • ♙ Pawn Picket — cheap, quick, everywhere. Checker Town's front line
  • ♘ Argus Outpost — Argus the guard-dog's kennel; splash damage hits the whole pack
  • ♗ Bishop Spire — long sightline; picks runners off from afar
  • ♖ Rook Bastion — slow, heavy castle-wall fire that flattens tanks
  • ♕ Queen's Guard — the premium royal escort: fast, far, and splashes

Twelve waves stand between the CEO and Chess City. Your final score logs to your operative dossier and the leaderboards — survive the King to top the board.
