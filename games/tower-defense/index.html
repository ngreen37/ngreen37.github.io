How to Play

  • From the menu, pick a map (beat one to unlock the next) — or open the 🏛 Shop to spend City Crowns on permanent upgrades
  • Place a defender by the road; the ♗ Bishop Sniper drops anywhere with a clear view (the preview turns red when obstructed). Tap a tower to set its aim (first / last / strong / weak), upgrade, or sell
  • Hold every wave to the King. Charge the 👑 Royal Decree to wipe the screen — lose all 50 city hearts and the gates fall

Four fronts stand between the CEO and Chess City — the Journey Road, the Sand Mine Pass, the Shogi Island Crossing, and the Chess City Gates. Crowns and your score persist to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
