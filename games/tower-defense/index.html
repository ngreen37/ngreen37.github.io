How to Play

  • From the menu, pick a map (beat one to unlock the next) — or open the 🏛 Shop to spend City Crowns on permanent upgrades
  • Place a defender by the road; the ♗ Bishop Sniper drops anywhere with a clear view (the preview turns red when obstructed). Tap a tower to set its aim (first / last / strong / weak), upgrade, or sell
  • Hold every wave to the King. Charge the 👑 Royal Decree to wipe the screen — lose all 50 city hearts and the gates fall
  • NEW · ♾ Endless — toggle it on the map screen for an unending survival run; how many waves you hold posts to the global Siege · Endless board. Add mutators (🌫 War Fog, ⚡ Forced March, ⛓ No Retreat) for bonus Crowns
  • NEW defenders & bosses — the ❄ Frost Knight chills the whole pack (low damage, big slow). Boss Kings summon reinforcements, and enemy Queens blink past your line — plan for both
  • NEW · ✦ Tower synergies — stand a Knight beside a Queen and it fires with haste; stand a Bishop beside a Rook and its beam pierces every foe in line. A pulsing ring marks an active pairing
  • NEW · 📅 Daily Siege — a mutator-roulette on a fixed daily seed (same map + mutators for everyone); your score posts to the global Siege · Daily board

Four fronts stand between the CEO and Chess City — the Journey Road, the Sand Mine Pass, the Shogi Island Crossing, and the Chess City Gates. Crowns and your score persist to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
