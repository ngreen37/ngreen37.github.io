How to Play

  • Move: point or drag (mouse / finger) — or WASD / arrow keys. Your runner heads where you point.
  • Tag Crockett to catch him for a belly rub — +1 🦴. He bolts off and you do it again.
  • Watch for his zoomies: every so often he darts in a burst and is briefly too fast to catch head-on — cut off his angles instead.
  • The bathroom is off-limits. He'll dodge it on his own, but if you herd him in he's BUSTED — a time-out, and +2 to you.
  • You've got 60 seconds. Stack up the bellies.

A quiet evening in the Checker Town apartment — except Crockett (the crew's real dog; the greeting is "bellies") has the zoomies. A light, fast chase to balance the chess. Your best score saves to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
