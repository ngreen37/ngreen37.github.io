Map. Tap one of your holdings, then a bordering enemy one. You need a garrison of 2 or more to attack. "Hold the line" ends your round without attacking.

Battle. Tap a piece, then a dot. One shared two-minute clock. Checkmate wins outright; on time, the most material standing wins and a tie goes to the defender.

The dice. Attacker rolls up to three, defender up to two. The totals are not compared — they are spent, at real chess values: queen 9, rook 5, bishop or knight 3, pawn 1. Kings are free.
