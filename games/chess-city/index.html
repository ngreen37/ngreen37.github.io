How to Play

  • Move: ← → or A / D  ·  Jump: ↑ / W / Space  ·  Fire: X / J. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.
  • The pieces each fight like they move: pawns swarm, knights hop erratically, bishops snipe on the diagonal, and rooks telegraph then charge in a straight line.
  • Bigger pieces take more hits. Mind the gaps in the road.
  • A Queen guards the end of the block — drop her to clear it.
  • Five hearts. Kills + distance = your score.
