How to Play

  • Pick a deck, choose 📖 Run-through (a whole opening from move 1) or 🃏 Spot cards (one position at a time)
  • Princess plays only book moves; on your turn, answer with the book move — click the piece, then its square
  • Stuck? 🐾 Show me reveals it and sends that line back for another pass

Every line is a real, named opening — the Pirc Defense, Italian Game, and Scandinavian, each with its main variations. Moves follow standard opening theory (cross-check any line in the lichess opening explorer). Master a line and spaced repetition rests it; slip and it returns sooner. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
