How to Play

  • Pick a deck, then Start Review — only the lines that are due come up
  • Princess plays her move; when it's your turn, answer with the book move: click the piece, then its square
  • Castling: click the king, then its destination (two squares over)
  • Stuck? Hit 🐾 Show me — but it counts as a slip
  • Finish a whole line cleanly and it's promoted — you'll see it again later
  • Slip up and it drops back to box 1 — it'll return much sooner
  • Your progress saves in this browser. Cram ignores the schedule for free practice

Decks

  • Pirc Defense (Black) — the Rival's signature: Classical, Austrian Attack, 150 Attack
  • Italian Game (White) — Giuoco Pianissimo & Giuoco Piano
  • Scandinavian (Black) — Main Line & the 3...Nf6 Modern

Spaced repetition is the same trick Anki uses: master something and it leaves you alone; fumble it and it keeps coming back until it sticks. Princess already knows these lines cold — your job is to keep up with her.

🧭 Coach me through it (optional)
  1. Start with the Italian deck if you're new — its moves are the most intuitive.
  2. Princess moves first each turn. Watch her move land, then it's you.
  3. To answer: click the piece you want to move, then click its destination square. The board only accepts the one book move.
  4. Truly stuck? 🐾 Show me reveals it — it just sends that line back to box 1 so you'll see it again sooner. That's the system working, not a failure.
  5. Don't grind everything at once — do the due lines, come back tomorrow, and let the schedule do the remembering for you.
