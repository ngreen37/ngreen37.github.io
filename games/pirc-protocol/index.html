How to Play

  • Pick a deck, choose 📖 Run-through (a whole opening from move 1) or 🃏 Spot cards (one position at a time)
  • Princess plays only book moves; on your turn, answer with the book move — click the piece, then its square
  • 👑 Boss Exam mixes spot cards from every variation at once; each line reveals its ECO code + a master-game citation on the back
  • ⬇ PGN exports your mastered lines; 🐾 Show me reveals a move and sends that line back for another pass
  • ☠ Blunder Traps — a deck of famous traps (Légal's, Scholar's, Fool's, From's Gambit). The opponent plays the losing move; you deliver the punishment. Every refutation is a machine-verified forced checkmate — proved to mate against every defence

Every line is a real, named opening — the Pirc Defense, Italian Game, and Scandinavian, with their main variations and ECO codes. Moves follow standard theory (cross-check any line in the lichess opening explorer). Master a line and spaced repetition rests it; slip and it returns sooner. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
