How to Play

  • Answer multiple-choice questions on chess and the PJCC files
  • Click an answer or use keys 1–4; Enter for the next question
  • Every correct answer earns clearance — climb RECRUIT → DELTA → GAMMA → BETA → OMEGA → ABOVE OMEGA
  • Each promotion unredacts a dossier file from the archive
  • Questions get harder as your clearance rises; build a streak for bonus points
  • Three wrong answers and your access is revoked

Styled after the Classified archive's clearance system. Chess rules and tactics sit at the lower clearances; the deepest PJCC secrets — the crash, the mines, Subject Zero — are Omega and above.
