How to Play

  • Answer multiple-choice questions on chess and the PJCC files — click or press 1–4. Some show a board diagram ("find the mate / name the tactic")
  • Each correct answer earns clearance: RECRUIT → DELTA → GAMMA → BETA → OMEGA → ABOVE OMEGA, every promotion unsealing a dossier file. Three wrong and access is revoked
  • Pick a focus — Rules · Tactics · History & Champions · PJCC — or run ⚡ Lightning (beat the per-question clock for bonus points)
  • ★ Daily is one shared 10-question exam — same for every operative; missed questions are logged on your Dossier to review

Set in the Invitational hall, styled after the Classified archive's clearance system. Your score and highest clearance log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
