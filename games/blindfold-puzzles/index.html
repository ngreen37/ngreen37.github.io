How to Play

  • The board is blank — catch the 🔦 and sweep its beam to read the clue, then click the piece that moves and its target square
  • Puzzles adapt — solve clean and they sharpen, slip and they ease (with wide variance, so it never spikes)
  • Switch modes: ⚡ Speed-Vision (shorter beam, combo multiplier), ⌨ Describe-only (no board — type the move in algebraic), the new 🔊 Audio-only mode (no board, no text — the position is spoken aloud with a coach mnemonic; type your answer, hit 🔊 Replay to hear it again), or the 📅 Daily shared position. Per-motif accuracy and your misses log to the Dossier
  • Solve enough and The Mind's Eye opens: a full game vs the CEO, played blind — pick a difficulty tier (Intern → CEO → Board of Directors), flip on 🔊 Narrate moves to hear each move called, try the blitz clock or the no-peek purist run for bonus credits, and replay your won game move by move

The final test of board vision — no pieces, just coordinates and your mind's eye. Progress logs to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
