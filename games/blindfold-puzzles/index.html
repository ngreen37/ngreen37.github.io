How to Play

  • The board is blank — you get only a written clue describing where every piece sits
  • Picture the position, then click the square of the piece that moves to reveal it
  • Then click the square it should move to — the winning move
  • Reveal position shows everything if you're stuck (counts as a slip)
  • Six puzzles per set — solve them clean to keep your score

The final test of board vision: no pieces, just coordinates and your mind's eye. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
