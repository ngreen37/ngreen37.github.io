How to Play

  • Pick a deck, then a mode. Learn Japanese: Hiragana, Katakana, Kanji (×2), Everyday Words, Greetings & Phrases, and Chess & Shogi terms. Reverse — for Japanese speakers learning English: English Words & English Phrases (the card flips: a Japanese prompt, an English answer).
  • 🔁 Review is the Anki loop that actually teaches you: see a character, recall it, tap Show answer, then grade yourself Again / Good / Easy. Cards you know come back later; cards you miss come back sooner.
  • ⚡ Quiz is the arcade layer: pick the reading or meaning from four choices, build a combo, and post a score to the leaderboards.
  • Your schedule is saved on this device, so you can study a little every day. Cram All drills a whole deck without touching the schedule.

Your teacher is Kaede — from Shogi Island, in Checker Town on an exchange program, who opened the Reading Room (読書室). She speaks Japanese first and translates second, so you learn by living in it. And Auston — the first to pull up a chair at Kaede's table — studies right alongside you. ゆっくりで いいよ — slow is just fine.
