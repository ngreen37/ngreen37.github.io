How to Play

  • Move: ← → or A / D. On a phone, use the on-screen D-pad.
  • Jump: ↑, W, or Space — mind the gaps in the road.
  • Shoot: X, J, or the FIRE button. Drop the grinning chess pieces before they reach you.
  • Bigger pieces take more hits. A Queen at the end of each block won't go quietly.
  • Three hits and Jerry's done. Distance + kills = your score.

Jerry Murphy — the pessimist of the family — is certain Chess City is overrun with evil chess pieces: pieces with mouths and eyes, waiting behind the gates. Everyone says he's paranoid. In his game, he's right. ("The pessimist was right.") Your best score saves to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
