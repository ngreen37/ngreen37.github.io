How to play. Move like a knight — tap a glowing dot. The tile you leave caves in; the red-outlined squares are covered by a piece, so dodge the crosshairs and dig deep. Land on a piece to capture it for gold — chain captures without taking a hit for a growing streak bonus (and a patch-up every third). Got a shovel? Press SPACE (or tap 🪏), then tap a caved-in pit to fill it back in.
