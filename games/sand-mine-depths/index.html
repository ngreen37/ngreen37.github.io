How to Play

  • Princess moves like a knight — only in L-shapes. Tap a glowing dot to hop
  • Reach the swirling shaft ▼ to descend to the next floor (10m deeper each time)
  • The tile you leave caves into a hole — plan your route so you don't trap yourself
  • Land on an enemy piece to capture it; grab gold ⛏ for score
  • Red squares are threatened — don't end your hop where a piece can reach you
  • Enemies move by chess rules after each hop. Three hearts and the mine claims you

The Sand Mines are where the Father works — and what he "has seen down there and will not discuss." The deeper you go, the more the mine remembers. Watch the messages at each new depth.
