How to Play

  • Princess moves like a knight — tap a glowing dot to hop. The tile you leave caves in; a red crosshair marks a square a piece can strike
  • Pawns march ▼ down and only strike diagonally — park right in front to pin one. Grab gold ⛏ and glinting relics ✦ (longer reach, lantern oil, a 🪏 shovel that fills a pit)
  • The deeper you go, the darker it gets — your torch only reaches so far. Beware Subject Zero: a rival knight that hunts you and digs through caved sand
  • Surface Camp banks your gold between runs for lasting gear; the Weekly Race is one seeded mine — same for everyone, perk-free

The Sand Mines are where the Father works — and what he "has seen down there and will not discuss." The deeper you go, the more the mine remembers; watch the message at each new depth. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
