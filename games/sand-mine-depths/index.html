How to play. Move like a knight — tap a glowing dot. The tile you leave caves in; the red-outlined squares are covered by a piece, so dodge the crosshairs and dig deep. Land on a piece to capture it for gold — chain captures without taking a hit for a growing streak bonus (and a patch-up every third).

The shovel. Those caved-in pits can wall you off from the exit ▼. When no hop can reach it, a warning appears and the 🪏 button pulses: press SPACE (or tap 🪏) to arm it, then tap a pit to fill it back in and reopen a path. No shovel? The sand eventually caves in on its own — costing a heart.
