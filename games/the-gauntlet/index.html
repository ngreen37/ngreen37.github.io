The Climb

It starts in Checker Town, at the bottom of everything, across a rough sea from the city that has what you want. Ten champions stand between you and the top of the tallest building in Chess City — the CEO's tower. Beat one, and the next door opens.

You begin against Argus, Checker Town's own champion, and climb through the Sand Mines, the crossing, and Shogi Isle to the city gates — then floor by floor past the CEO's cronies: the Auditor, the Enforcer, the Vice President, your Rival — until only the CEO is left, alone at the top.

Every champion plays real, distinct chess on a tunable, perft-verified engine — no two the same. You alternate colors as you climb. It's strict: no takebacks, no hints. Results post to the leaderboards.

A draw isn't enough — the door only opens on a win. And they say the climb doesn't quite end at the top… not for anyone who reaches it.
