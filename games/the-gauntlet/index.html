How to Play

  • Open The Ladder and pick your challenger — beat one to unlock the next.
  • Tap a piece to see its legal moves (green dots), then tap a square to move. A pawn reaching the last rank prompts a promotion choice.
  • You alternate colors as you climb (your color shows top-right). It's strict — no takebacks, no hints.
  • Climb from the Checker-Town Recruit through Argus the Guard-Dog, the City Gatekeeper, and the Rival, to the CEO at the gates. Results post to the leaderboards.

Each champion plays a real, distinct style — the recruit grabs everything and blunders; Argus hunts your king; the Rival and the CEO simply don't miss. The engine plays full legal chess (castling, en passant, promotion) and is perft-verified.
