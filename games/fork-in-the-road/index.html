How to Play

  • Click a piece, then its target square to play the winning move — a fork or a mate
  • Journey is 1,000 adaptive steps to Chess City; Daily Five is a shared seeded set
  • Stuck? 🐾 Hint nudges once, then reveals (it breaks your streak)

Puzzles are generated and verified by construction, scaling to your skill — solve and they sharpen, slip and they ease off (with enough variance that it never spikes). Journey progress saves to your operative dossier; scores hit the leaderboards.
