How to Play

  • Click a piece, then its target square to play the winning move — a fork, skewer, or mate
  • Journey: 1,000 adaptive steps to Chess City, with a boss guarding each region gate. Daily Ladder: one seeded climb, the same for everyone
  • Stuck? 🐾 Hint tiers up — show the piece → name the motif → reveal the move (each costs your clean solve)

Every puzzle is generated and verified by construction (forks, skewers, and mates — checked sound, not guessed). A card after each solve names the motif so you actually learn it, and your per-tactic accuracy lands on your operative dossier; scores hit the leaderboards.
