How to Play

  • Each puzzle shows whose move it is and the goal (win material or mate)
  • Click a piece to see its moves, then click the target square
  • Find the winning move — forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, mates
  • Multi-move puzzles play the opponent's reply automatically — keep the combination going
  • Stuck? 🐾 Hint nudges you once, then reveals the move (it breaks your streak)
  • Each solve walks Princess one step closer to Chess City

Modes

  • Journey — solve your way across the world map, Checker Town to Chess City
  • Daily Five — five puzzles in a fixed order that changes each day

Princess is designated a knight ♞, so the bank leans into knight forks — the trickiest pattern for new players to see coming.
