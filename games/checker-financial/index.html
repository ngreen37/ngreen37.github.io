How to Play

  • A trade crosses the wire: the pieces you'd give vs. the pieces you'd get
  • Call it before the clock runs out — ▲ Profit, = Even, or ▼ Loss for you
  • Piece values: ♟1   ♞3   ♝3   ♜5   ♛9
  • Keys: 1 profit, 2 even, 3 loss
  • Right calls bank profit and build a bull-run multiplier; the timer speeds up as you climb
  • Three bad calls and the market closes

Named for the paper the Father reads "even though he has no money — to find the REAL news." Counting material is the most useful habit a new player can build: know what a trade is actually worth before you make it.
