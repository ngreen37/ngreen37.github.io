How to Play

  • Drag or move to fly Princess — she fires automatically. Don't let the pieces slip past the bottom (each leak costs a heart)
  • Pop pieces for power-ups: ♗ Bishop = an X-shot · ♘ Knight = two sharpshooter escorts · ♕ Queen = a piercing super-shot
  • Build a combo to charge your ♚ King's summon — press Space (or the button) to hurl pawns that leap into the swarm and explode
  • Every region ends in a boss King; fly town to town to Chess City… and the secret office beyond it

The whole PJCC journey — Checker Town to Chess City — reimagined as a chess-Bloons shooter, with the CEO's Office waiting at the very top. Built in canvas and designed to port to the Blender/Godot 3D path later. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
