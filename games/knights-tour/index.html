How to Play

  • Princess moves like a knight — L-shapes only. Tap a glowing square to hop
  • Visit every square exactly once to cross a region; you can never land on a square twice
  • ↶ Undo steps back if you paint yourself into a corner
  • 🐾 Hint uses the knight's old trick — head for the square with the fewest exits left
  • Five regions, growing from 5×5 to a full 8×8, trace the road from Checker Town to Chess City

The Knight's Tour is a classic chess puzzle going back centuries. Every board here is guaranteed to have a complete tour from the starting corner — the hint will always find one if you get stuck.
