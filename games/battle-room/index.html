How to Play

  • You're White — Princess at the controller. Checkmate the Swarm.
  • Controller: the on-screen D-pad (or arrow keys) moves Princess's cursor; A (Enter / Z) selects a piece, then launches the move; B (Esc / X) cancels. You can also just tap a square.
  • Every move plays out as an action sequence in the battle room up top — remember Ender's mantra: the enemy's gate is down. The 2D board stays live on the side so you can always read the position.
  • Win fast and capture material for a higher score — it posts to the leaderboards.

A v0.1 prototype: real, legal chess on the same perft-verified engine as The Gauntlet, wrapped in PJCC's signature view-shift from the 2D board to a full action sequence and back. Built to be decked out later in Godot + Blender — 3D piece-fighters, true camera cuts, and voiced commentators.
