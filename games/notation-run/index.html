How to Play

  • A square name slides in on the beat — click that square when it reaches the gate
  • Sharper timing scores more; a wrong square breaks your combo

Pick a tempo — Slow, Medium, or Fast, each with its own colour theme — and read the board fast. Hit a coordinate dead-on the gate for a Perfect; drift early or late and it scores Great, Good, or less; miss it and it tumbles off the cliff. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards. And they say a flawless run at the highest tempo unlocks something that takes the pieces away entirely…
