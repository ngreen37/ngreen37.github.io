How to Play

  • Square names (calls) slide in from the right on the beat
  • When a call reaches the gate, click that square on the board
  • Or type it — file then rank (e.g. e then 4)
  • Perfect / Good depend on your timing — early or late both count
  • Wrong square breaks your combo and reveals the right one
  • Read the a–h files and 1–8 ranks on the board edges

Tempo

  • Stroll — gentle pace to learn the board
  • March — the standard beat
  • Sprint — fast calls for sharp board vision

Pure coordinate drilling — read the files and ranks fast. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards.
