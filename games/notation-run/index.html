How to Play

  • A square name slides in on the beat — click that square when it reaches the gate
  • Sharper timing scores more; a wrong square breaks your combo

Pick a tempo — Stroll, March, or Sprint — and read the board fast. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards. And they say a flawless run at the highest tempo unlocks something that takes the pieces away entirely…
