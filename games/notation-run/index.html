How to Play

  • A square name slides in on the beat — click that square at the gate. Dead-on = Perfect; drift = Great/Good; if it slips past, catch it on the tail before the cliff
  • Drill is a fixed run; Endless ramps the tempo until you run out of hearts. Set any speed with the BPM slider, and try ⬛⬜ Black side — the board flips and goes 2-Tone
  • ⟲ Extreme keeps flipping the board between the White and Black views every few beats — the theme shifts and a "FACING WHITE / BLACK" chip tells you which way you're oriented. Keep up if you can

Your timing trend tracks whether you're drifting early or late across runs. Scores log to your operative dossier and the leaderboards — and a flawless Fast run still unlocks something that takes the pieces away entirely…
