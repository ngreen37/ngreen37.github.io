How to Play

  • ← → or A / D — change lane (while airborne)
  • SPACE or W — jump from one piece to the next
  • Land on a chess-piece track and you lock on, sliding to its end automatically
  • Each piece forms its own connected track — rooks run long and straight, bishops zigzag diagonally, knights cut an L
  • Purple King tracks have only one safe lane — the red ✕ lanes will cost you HP
  • Queen tracks give a bonus heart
  • Miss a track entirely and you lose HP. Three hearts and you're out.

The thrower is the CEO of the Chess City Invitational — the one who sent Princess on her journey in the first place. That's his companion beside him.
