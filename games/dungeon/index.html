How to Play

  • You move like a King — one step in any direction. Tap a green dot to move; tap yourself to wait a turn.
  • Every enemy is a chess piece that moves by its own rules. Squares they attack glow red — end your turn on one and you'll be captured, costing a heart.
  • Step onto an enemy to capture it. Reach the stairs ▟ at the top to clear the floor and choose a relic.
  • Relics stack: Bishop Dash, Rook Rush, and the Knight's Blink turn Princess into a hybrid piece. Go as deep as you can — results post to the leaderboards.

This is the 2D proving ground for the Godot 3D crawler — the same core (piece-rule enemies, threat-reading, move-set relics, biome floors from Checker Town to Chess City) that the full build will render in three dimensions.
