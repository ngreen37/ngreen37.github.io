Read the threatened squares, find the safe path, capture what you can, and reach the stairs. Earn stacking move-relics that build Princess into a hybrid piece. The web prototype for the Godot 3D build." permalink: /games/dungeon/ ---

How to play. Move one step like a King — tap a green dot. Dodge the red (attacked) squares, capture enemies, and reach the stairs ▟. Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Q·E·Z·C for diagonals, Space waits.
