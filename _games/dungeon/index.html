How to Play

  • Arrow keys or WASD — move Princess
  • Move onto an enemy to capture it
  • Enemy pieces move by chess rules — pawns advance, knights L-shape, bishops diagonal
  • Red squares show threatened tiles — avoid them
  • Clear all enemies → EXIT opens top-right corner
  • Enemy count and piece types scale each floor
