# Festivals & Grants — funding/reach target list (Avenue 10 · #4)

A working planning tracker for getting PJCC in front of the people who fund and platform indie
animation. **This is an internal planning doc, not a published page.** Deadlines, fees, and
eligibility shift every year — **verify each one before you act on it.** Treat this as the map, not
the schedule.

What you can submit *now*, even pre-pilot:
- **The Pilot animatic** (page pulled from the live site 2026-07-07; restore from git) — a storyboard reel with scratch VO counts as a
  work-in-progress / pitch piece for many "in development" and pitch tracks.
- **A teaser / proof-of-concept** clip when the first Blender cutscene exists.
- **The series bible** (the Lore Codex + characters + locations, exported) as a pitch packet.

## 🎬 Festivals (animation-friendly; many have "in development" / pitch tracks)

| Festival | Why it fits | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **Annecy (MIFA)** | The animation industry's center of gravity; MIFA Pitches has a TV-series category. | Pitch-track deadlines hit early; entry fees + a strong pitch deck required. |
| **Ottawa International Animation Festival (OIAF)** | Major North American animation fest; industry "TAC" market. | Short/WIP categories; good for a teaser. |
| **Annecy's "Work in Progress"** | Built for unfinished projects. | Needs a credible reel + plan. |
| **Cartoon Forum (Cartoon, EU)** | THE pitching event for European TV animation co-production/finance. | EU-leaning; producer/co-pro oriented. |
| **SXSW** | Broad reach, indie/animation shorts + a buzzy crowd. | Competitive; better with a finished short. |
| **Sundance** | Prestige; episodic + shorts; huge platform value. | Long shot pre-pilot; aim a polished proof-of-concept. |
| **Tribeca** | Episodic + immersive; press reach. | Short/episodic tracks. |
| **GLAS, Fantoche, Zagreb, Hiroshima** | Respected art-animation fests; credibility + community. | Mostly finished shorts. |

**Strategy:** prioritize **pitch/WIP tracks** (Annecy MIFA, Cartoon Forum, OIAF market) over
finished-film competitions while pre-pilot — those exist specifically to fund things that aren't done.

## 💰 Grants & funds (verify region/eligibility — many are country-specific)

| Source | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Regional/state arts councils** | Project grants | Often the most accessible; tied to where you live. Start here. |
| **National arts endowments** | Project / media arts | e.g. NEA (US), Canada Council / Telefilm (CA), Creative Europe MEDIA (EU). Check eligibility. |
| **Documentary/animation-specific funds** | Project | Some media-arts funds cover animated nonfiction/hybrid. |
| **Sundance / film-org artist grants** | Development | Competitive; development money for early projects. |
| **Crowdfunding (Kickstarter / Backerkit)** | Direct | Not a grant, but the most controllable "fund the pilot" lever — pairs with Avenue 4 (chess set, art book, pins). |
| **Platform/brand creator funds** | Sponsorship | Chess platforms, edu brands, animation-tool makers (see partnerships below). |

**Honest take:** most formal grants are **region-locked and slow**. The realistic near-term money is
**(1) direct support (Patreon / the dispatch)**, **(2) a Kickstarter tied to a physical product**, and
**(3) regional arts council** project grants. Festivals are for **reach and credibility** more than
cash, pre-pilot.

## 🤝 Partnership targets (Avenue 10 · #8 — *relationship work, not a build*)

These require **you** to reach out; the site can only make you look ready (that's what `/press-kit/`
is for). Categories + the angle for each:

- **Chess platforms** (Lichess, Chess.com) — angle: cartoon-led chess education for kids; the Academy
  could feed/embed. Lichess is open-source/nonprofit and partnership-friendly.
- **Chess streamers / educators** (YouTube/Twitch) — angle: a charming, free chess arcade + a story
  hook; offer a featured cameo or a custom puzzle pack.
- **Schools / educators** — angle: the Academy as a free, kid-safe, offline classroom tool; a
  pilot-classroom kit.
- **Animation-tool makers** (Blender Foundation community, etc.) — angle: a made-in-public case study.
- **Podcast networks** (if the audio drama ships) — angle: a ready-made fiction-podcast pilot.

**Outreach kit to build when you're ready:** a one-page PDF (logline, 3 stills, the numbers, the ask)
— I can generate it from `/press-kit/` on request.
