How it works

1. Pick a fight. Tap one of your holdings, then a neighbouring enemy one. You need a garrison of 2 or more to attack.

2. Both sides roll. Attacker rolls up to three dice, defender up to two — Risk rules. The totals aren't compared. They're spent.

3. The dice buy an army. Every point buys chess material at its real value: queen 9, rook 5, bishop or knight 3, pawn 1. Kings are free — they always turn up.

4. Two minutes. One shared clock. Checkmate wins outright. If the clock runs out, the most material standing wins, and a tie goes to the defender — ground is easier held than taken.

5. The winner takes the holding. Lose them all and the campaign is over.
