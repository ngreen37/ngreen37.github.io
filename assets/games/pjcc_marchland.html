CHESSWILD

Campaign

Ten holdings. Take them all.
The dice decide who shows up.
The board decides who wins.

A fair fight that punishes carelessness.

v0.2 · prototype

How it works

1. Three parts to your round. Deploy every new troop, attack up to three times, fortify once. Then you watch theirs.

2. Three attacks a round. Tap your holding, then a bordering enemy one — you need two troops. Which three is the whole decision.

3. Troops are dice. One die per two troops, up to four, and you keep the best. Under three troops you cannot field a queen.

4. Chain your holdings. Two or more neighbors of your own and it is chained — an extra die every time it defends.

5. Two dice a battle: material, then position. Material buys your army at real chess value — queen 9, rook 5, bishop or knight 3, pawn 1. Kings are free.

6. Position places it. High rolls march an attacker's pawns up the board. Defending, 7 or better tucks your king into the corner with a pawn in front of him — and an 8 brings his rook around and three pawns with him.

7. Fifteen seconds each, a second back on every move. Run your own clock out and you lose. Checkmate takes the ground. A draw goes to the defender.

8. Take every holding to win. Lose them all and the campaign is over.
