Separates Checker Town from Chess City

## Massive, unyielding
- massive
- unyielding

## Who Lives Here
There are humans, or maybe-human like amphibious creatures that live there

## Mysteries
There is a chessboard there where important games are played.  What Nate doesn't know is that the frame can drop out, causing each individual square to bob and float in the middle of the sea

## Role in the Story
It is the setting of a great challenge, when playing on the sea chessboard.  Throughout the story, it represents the divide between Checker Town and Chess City.
