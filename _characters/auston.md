Auston is the main character's younger sister — two years younger, and about ten years braver than that math should allow. Where her brother is all forward motion and big dreams, Auston is curious, fearless, and quietly the sharpest pattern-spotter in the family.

## Traits
- Two years younger than the main character
- Bright and relentlessly curious — asks the question everyone else was too proud to
- Fearless in a way that worries the people who love her
- Took one look at the island's puzzle game, **shogi**, and never looked back
- Adores her big brother, and isn't shy about telling him when he's wrong

## Background
With their father kind but scattered and often away with the work crew, it was the main character who quietly stepped up. He learned to braid hair, to pack a lunch, to sit up on the bad nights. Somewhere along the way the older-brother role grew into something more like a father's — and Auston grew up steady because of it.

She pays him back in her own currency: she believes in him completely, and she keeps him honest. When the whole world tells the main character to be reasonable, Auston is the one who says *go*.

## Role in the Story
Auston is the heart that raises the stakes. The journey to Chess City stops being only about the main character's ambition the moment you understand he is also doing it for **her** — to build a life big enough for a little sister who deserves the whole board. Their bond is the show's warmest engine: a brother who became a father, and the sister who made him better at both.

In the games, Auston runs the **Shogi Dojo** at the Academy — the island's game became her game first.
