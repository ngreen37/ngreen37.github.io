**Matsu** is **Kaede's older brother** — a fox from **Shogi Island** who, unlike his sister, never left. Where Kaede is the maple (楓) that changes with every season, Matsu is the **pine (松): evergreen, unmoved, the same in every weather.** He is the keeper of the island's oldest games and the quiet counterweight to Kaede's wandering.

## Traits
- Steady and laconic — says in five words what Kaede says in fifty
- A formidable **shogi** player; teaches by playing, not explaining
- Believes some things are meant to be *stayed with*, not carried away
- Plays a **knight** — the one piece that moves by leaping, like a fox
- Proud of his sister's road, even as he guards the one she left

## Background
Matsu and Kaede grew up on Shogi Island, where the pieces wear their names on their faces. When the crew passed through and Kaede followed the Sea back to Checker Town to open the Reading Room, Matsu stayed — tending the dojo, the docks, and the island's half-forgotten rites. He and Kaede are two answers to the same question: *do you carry the old world outward, or keep it alive where it stands?*

## Role in the Story
A **side character** and the Shogi Island anchor — the reason the island feels lived-in rather than a stop on a map. He's the bridge to its deeper rules (and, perhaps, to the [locked second shelf](/characters/kaede/) of scrolls Kaede can't yet read). Where Kaede teaches you to *read* the island, Matsu teaches you to *respect* it.

## A word from Matsu
- 「ゆっくり来い。」 — *Yukkuri koi.* — "Come slowly."
- 「松は枯れない。」 — *Matsu wa karenai.* — "The pine does not wither."
