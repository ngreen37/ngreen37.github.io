The **three commentators** are the voice of the tournaments — the booth that calls the big matches from up in the rafters. (when it's ready) They'll have the mics for **[the Gauntlet](/games/the-gauntlet/)** too. 

## The booth
- **Randy — play-by-play.** All energy, no brakes. Calls every capture like it's the last move on Earth. 
- **Chipper — color commentator.** Dry, precise, faintly amused. Explains *why* the move was brilliant a half-second before Boomer realizes it was. 
- **Pittsy — Down by the board** Watches the *people*, not the pieces. Finds the story in every match — the nerves, the comebacks, the underdog stories. 

## Role in the Story
The commentators turn a chess game into an **spectacle** — they're the connective tissue between the games and the show: their banter is where rules get explained, further plot lines, and add comic relief.
