A fox from Shogi Island who crossed the Sea and settled in Checker Town, where she runs a tiny lending library called **the Reading Room (読書室)** and teaches anyone who wanders in how to read the old written language — one character at a time.

## Traits
- Patient, warm, and unhurried — she believes nobody is "bad at" anything, only early in their practice
- Speaks Japanese first and translates second, so you pick it up by living in it
- Keeps a brush, an inkstone, and an absurd number of paper scraps for drilling characters
- Plays as a bishop in games — she sees the board from an angle no one else does
- Named for the maple (楓); she says a single leaf taught her that small things change a whole season

## Background
Kaede grew up on **Shogi Island**, where the pieces wear their names on their faces and reading is the whole game. When the journey's crew passed through, she followed the Sea back the other way and made a home in **Checker Town** — a place that had games everywhere but had half-forgotten how to *read* them. So she opened the Reading Room: free to enter, free to borrow, and free to sit while she teaches you your **kana** and your first **kanji**.

## Role in the Story
Kaede is the bridge between Checker Town and Shogi Island — the friendly face of a language and a world the crew doesn't understand yet. She's the in-world teacher behind **[The Reading Room](/games/reading-room/)**, the spaced-repetition trainer where you actually learn to read along with her. Where Argus teaches steadiness and the Dad teaches the long game, Kaede teaches that understanding is just practice you haven't done yet.

## A word from Kaede
- 「ようこそ、読書室へ。」 — *Yōkoso, dokushoshitsu e.* — "Welcome to the Reading Room."
- 「ゆっくりで いいよ。」 — *Yukkuri de ii yo.* — "Slow is just fine."
- 「もう一回。」 — *Mō ikkai.* — "One more time."
