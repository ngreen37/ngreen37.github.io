**Maxwell** is Nate's oldest friend -- and in some ways, a great obstacle. Not through malice, but through the quiet weight of complacency.

## Traits
- Has known main character since pre-school
- sometimes he seems to lack inspiration, sometimes speaks with great passion about improving life in Checker Town
- Makes fun of Nate's ambitions, but is not always able to express his true feelings

## Background
Growing up together in Checker Town, Maxwell and Nate were inseparable. But where Nate looks outward toward Chess City, Maxwell looks inward -- comfortable with what is, skeptical of what could be. 

## Role in the Story
Maxwell represents the pull of staying put -- the voice that says the dream isn't worth chasing. His presence gives Nate's journey its emotional stakes: leaving town means leaving him behind, too.
