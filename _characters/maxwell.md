**Maxwell** is the narrator's oldest friend -- and in many ways, his greatest obstacle. Not through malice, but through the quiet weight of complacency.

## Traits
- Has known the narrator since pre-school
- Uninspired, or simply content with life in Checker Town
- Makes fun of the narrator's ambitions, driven by his own insecurity
- Ultimately drifts into Michael's orbit, though for different reasons

## Background
Growing up together in Checker Town, Maxwell and the narrator were inseparable. But where the narrator looks outward toward Chess City, Maxwell looks inward -- comfortable with what is, skeptical of what could be. When the narrator is sent away, Maxwell and Michael form an unlikely team, each taking in one of the dogs left behind.

## Role in the Story
Maxwell represents the pull of staying put -- the voice that says the dream isn't worth chasing. His presence gives the narrator's journey its emotional stakes: leaving means leaving him behind too.
