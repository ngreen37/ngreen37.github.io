A pair of brothers. [Robert](/characters/robert/)**, the Checker Town rival — and the show's **prodigal sons.** They took their share of Robert's gospel-of-mindset, ran off to spend it on the wrong things, and learned the hard way that *belief without a heart behind it* spends fast. Their arc is the parable: away, undone, and (eventually) the long road back.

## The pair
- **The elder brother** — bought the swagger, skipped the substance. Loud certainty, thin follow-through. The cautionary version of Robert's mindset.
- **The younger brother** — softer, more lost than reckless. Followed his brother out the door and is the more likely of the two to turn around first.

## Role in the Story
Ancillary foils that give Robert's *mindset* philosophy a shadow: here is what "95% mindset" looks like when the 5% — and the love — is missing. They echo the show's larger theme of **true vs. counterfeit** (Michael's righteousness vs. the self-righteous fall), and they're a redemption thread the writers can pull on: the prodigal who comes home lands harder *because* you watched him leave. A possible beat from canon — **even they have redeeming qualities; perhaps they're the ones who save one of the dogs.**

> Prodigal by design: the point isn't the leaving, it's the coming back. Plant them now so the return pays off later.
