**Gerald Murphy** — *"Jerry"* to everyone — is the family pessimist. Nate's older cousin.  The surname fits: this is the family that lent its name to **Murphy's Law** (*whatever can go wrong, will*), and Jerry lives by it like scripture. Where the Uncle has his self-belief, and [Robert](/characters/robert/) carries the gospel of mindset, Jerry carries the dread: he is *certain* Chess City is no promised land but a nest of **evil chess pieces** — pieces with mouths and eyes, watching from behind the gates. Everyone calls him paranoid. In **[his game](/games/murphys-law/)**, he's right.

## Traits
- His worry is a real radar: it **catches threats other people walk straight into** — the loose plank, the ambush, the trap that smiles.
- The same worry is a blind spot; his head down bracing for the hit
- Speaks in warnings and worst-cases; his catchphrase is a grim little *"...called it!"*
- Plays as an **f-pawn** 

## Background
Jerry grew up on the same Checker Town streets as his cousins, but, unlike his more gregarious family members, he is one of the wary.  He didn't choose fear; fear chose him and chose him early, and it has, on occasion, been *correct* — which is exactly what makes it so hard to put down. 

## Role in the Story
Jerry represents that inner sense of dread one gets in certain situations — the shadow side of the optimism the rest of the journey runs on. His comedy and his heart come from the same gag: his worst fears are **vindicated on-screen** (the pieces really are grinning at him) while the world around him stays stubbornly warm. He's the playable lead of **[Murphy's Law](/games/murphys-law/)**, the Mega-Man-style scroller whose tagline says it all — *"The pessimist was right."* His arc is the slow, tender argument that being right about the danger is not the same as being right about the world.
