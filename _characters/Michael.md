**Michael** is the **Checker Town rival** — introduced as an antagonist, revealed to be one of the most important allies in the story, and the show's truest moral compass. His name carries its meaning because Michael is the character who, again and again, **acts truly righteous.** 

## Traits
- Same age as the narrator
- Intensely driven by chess — it is his only road back to his family
- Tough exterior that softens once common ground is found
- A skilled teacher; introduces Princess to the **Pirc Defense**

## Background
He lives in Checker Town, but his entire family is in Chess City. That separation is the engine behind everything he does. He and the narrator start as rivals but find that they want the same things — and Michael's instinct, every time, is to do right by the people around him.

## Role in the Story
When the narrator is sent away, Michael steps up — taking in Princess and continuing her chess education. His arc moves from adversary to unlikely cornerstone of the group. He is a **side character** whose quiet, costly righteousness sets the story's moral center.
